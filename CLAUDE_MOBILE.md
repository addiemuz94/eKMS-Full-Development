Also checked, not assumed: AID-conflict/default-app resolution only applies when two services register the *same* AID — since A and B use different AIDs under `category="other"`, both can be registered and enabled simultaneously with no forced system default; the in-screen enable/disable toggles are a testing convenience (`PackageManager.setComponentEnabledSetting`) rather than something the platform requires.
